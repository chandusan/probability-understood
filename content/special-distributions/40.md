# Solve all three hurricane probabilities with the correct endpoints

Assume the annual count is $X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(13.1)$. We now evaluate the two tail questions and the conditional probability, keeping enough precision to avoid carrying rounding errors into the final ratio.

## At most eight hurricanes

The allowed values are $0,1,\ldots,8$:

$$\boxed{P(X\le8)=\sum_{x=0}^8e^{-13.1}\frac{13.1^x}{x!}
\approx0.095272122.}$$

This is about 9.53%.

## At least ten hurricanes

The complement contains $0,1,\ldots,9$:

$$\begin{aligned}
P(X\ge10)&=1-P(X\le9)\\
&=1-\{P(X\le8)+P(X=9)\}\\
&=1-(0.095272122+0.064035516)\\
&=\boxed{0.840692362\text{ approximately}.}
\end{aligned}$$

To four decimal places this is $0.8407$. The source's $0.8400$ is a numerical error, not the correct four-decimal rounding.

## At most fifteen, given at least ten

Write the conditional probability as intersection divided by condition:

$$\begin{aligned}
P(X\le15\mid X\ge10)
&=\frac{P(\{X\le15\}\cap\{X\ge10\})}{P(X\ge10)}\\
&=\frac{P(10\le X\le15)}{P(X\ge10)}\\
&=\frac{\sum_{x=10}^{15}e^{-13.1}13.1^x/x!}{0.840692362}\\
&\approx\frac{0.595384641}{0.840692362}\\
&=\boxed{0.708207506.}
\end{aligned}$$

Thus the conditional probability is about 70.82%. The source's rounded value $0.708$ agrees to three decimals.

## Why P(X ≤ 15) is not the numerator

Counts from zero through nine satisfy $X\le15$ but violate the condition $X\ge10$. They must be removed before division. The numerator keeps precisely the outcomes meeting **both** requirements.

A useful check is that $0.595385\le0.840692$, so the conditional ratio lies between zero and one. The intersection cannot have more probability than the conditioning event containing it.
