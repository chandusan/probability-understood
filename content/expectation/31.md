# Solve all four parts of the three-value moment example

Let $X$ take values $1,2,3$ with probabilities $1/2,1/3,1/6$. Find its mean, standard deviation, third raw moment, and third central moment. The last two are different quantities: one cubes the values themselves, while the other cubes deviations from the mean.

## Organize the raw moments in one table

| $x$ | $p(x)$ | $xp(x)$ | $x^2p(x)$ | $x^3p(x)$ |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $1$ | $1/2$ | $1/2$ | $1/2$ | $1/2$ |
| $2$ | $1/3$ | $2/3$ | $4/3$ | $8/3$ |
| $3$ | $1/6$ | $1/2$ | $3/2$ | $9/2$ |

The probabilities sum to $1$. Adding the last three columns gives

$$
E(X)=\frac53,\qquad E(X^2)=\frac{10}{3},\qquad E(X^3)=\frac{23}{3}.
$$

Thus part (a) is $\boxed{\mu=5/3}$, and part (c) is $\boxed{\mu_3=23/3}$.

## Part (b): variance and standard deviation

The deviations from $5/3$ are $-2/3$, $1/3$, and $4/3$. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Var}(X)
&=\left(-\frac23\right)^2\frac12
+\left(\frac13\right)^2\frac13
+\left(\frac43\right)^2\frac16\\
&=\frac29+\frac1{27}+\frac8{27}
=\frac59.
\end{aligned}
$$

Take the nonnegative square root:

$$\boxed{\sigma=\frac{\sqrt5}{3}\approx0.7454.}$$

## Part (d): cube the deviations, including their signs

$$
\begin{aligned}
\mu_3^0
&=\left(-\frac23\right)^3\frac12
+\left(\frac13\right)^3\frac13
+\left(\frac43\right)^3\frac16\\
&=-\frac4{27}+\frac1{81}+\frac{32}{81}\\
&=\boxed{\frac7{27}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The answer is positive: large positive deviations contribute more than negative ones after cubing and weighting.

## Keep the requested answers distinct

| Quantity | Answer |
|---|---:|
| Mean | $5/3$ |
| Standard deviation | $\sqrt5/3$ |
| Third raw moment | $23/3$ |
| Third central moment | $7/27$ |

If skewness is also desired, divide the third central moment by $\sigma^3$: $\gamma_1=7/(5\sqrt5)$. This is an additional standardized quantity, not the requested third central moment itself.
