# Compare kurtosis using one clear convention

The familiar labels platykurtic, mesokurtic, and leptokurtic compare a distribution's standardized fourth moment with the normal value of three. In the excess-kurtosis convention, the corresponding reference is zero. The labels summarize one moment-based comparison, not every feature of a curve's shape.

## Put both conventions side by side

| Classification | Kurtosis $\beta_2$ | Excess kurtosis $\gamma_2=\beta_2-3$ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Platykurtic | Below $3$ | Negative |
| Mesokurtic | Equal to $3$ | Zero |
| Leptokurtic | Above $3$ | Positive |

A normal distribution is mesokurtic. Other distributions can also have kurtosis three, so this number alone does not prove normality.

## Use the uniform distribution as a concrete reference

For $X$ uniform on $[0,1]$,

$$\mu=\frac12,\qquad \sigma^2=\frac1{12},\qquad \mu_4^0=\frac1{80}.$$

Therefore

$$
\beta_2=\frac{1/80}{(1/12)^2}=\frac{144}{80}=\frac95,
\qquad
\gamma_2=\frac95-3=\boxed{-\frac65}.
$$

Its values are confined to a bounded interval. In standardized units they lie between $-\sqrt3$ and $\sqrt3$, so very large standardized deviations never occur. This is consistent with a smaller fourth-power average than the normal model.

## Why matching scales matters

A distribution measured in centimeters can have a much larger raw fourth central moment than the same distribution measured in meters. Dividing by $\sigma^4$ removes that unit dependence.

When comparing pictures, imagine both distributions recentered and scaled to standard deviation one. Otherwise a wider-looking curve might simply reflect different measurement units or variance, rather than different standardized shape.

## A practical limit of the description

High kurtosis means a large average fourth power of standardized deviations. It often accompanies influential extreme observations, but it does not specify exactly how much probability lies beyond every chosen threshold. Tail probabilities such as $P(|Z|>3)$ require their own calculation from the distribution.

The source's curves illustrate broad shape families. The numerical definition is the reliable basis for deciding which kurtosis category a particular model belongs to.
