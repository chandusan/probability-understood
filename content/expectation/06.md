# Solve the roulette exercise using net profit

Place a one-dollar bet on red. Assume every pocket is equally likely and a winning red bet pays even money: the player gains one dollar in net profit on red and loses one dollar otherwise. A European wheel has 18 red, 18 black, and one green pocket; an American wheel has 18 red, 18 black, and two green pockets.

## Define the random variable carefully

Let $Y$ be the player's **net profit** from the bet. Then $Y=1$ on red and $Y=-1$ on every other color. A win returns the original dollar plus one additional dollar, so a two-dollar cash return corresponds to only one dollar of profit.

## European wheel: 37 equally likely pockets

There are 18 winning and $18+1=19$ losing pockets. Thus

$$
P(Y=1)=\frac{18}{37},\qquad P(Y=-1)=\frac{19}{37}.
$$

Weight the two profits:

$$
E(Y)=1\frac{18}{37}+(-1)\frac{19}{37}
=\boxed{-\frac1{37}\text{ dollars}}\approx-0.0270.
$$

The expected loss is about 2.70 cents per dollar wagered. There is no outcome “lose 2.70 cents”; this describes the average over many comparable bets.

## American wheel: 38 equally likely pockets

Now there are 18 winning and 20 losing pockets:

$$
E(Y)=1\frac{18}{38}-1\frac{20}{38}
=\boxed{-\frac1{19}\text{ dollars}}\approx-0.0526.
$$

The expected loss is about 5.26 cents per dollar wagered. The extra green pocket increases the losing probability while leaving the winning payoff unchanged.

## The same calculation using cash returned

Let $R$ be total money returned by the casino: $R=2$ after a win and $R=0$ after a loss. For Europe,

$$
E(R)=2\frac{18}{37}=\frac{36}{37}.
$$

Subtract the one-dollar stake: $E(Y)=E(R)-1=-1/37$, exactly as before. Defining net profit first prevents the common mistake of assigning payoffs $+2$ and $-1$, which counts the stake inconsistently.

<details markdown="1"><summary>What would make the red bet fair?</summary>

For a European wheel, let the net winning profit be $w$. Fairness means $18w/37-19/37=0$, giving $w=19/18$ dollars per one-dollar stake. The actual even-money profit is only one dollar.
