# Conditioning means looking only inside the observed group

A conditional distribution answers how a random variable behaves after some information is known. We first keep only outcomes consistent with that information, then express probabilities relative to the total probability of that smaller group.

## Start with an ordinary fraction

Imagine 100 customers whose status and engagement match the given customer model. Exactly 20 have low engagement: 5 were retained and 15 churned. A customer is selected uniformly, and we are told that the customer has low engagement.

The information leaves **20 possible customers**, of whom **15 churned**. Hence

$$
P(\text{churned}\mid\text{low engagement})=\frac{15}{20}=0.75.
$$

The vertical bar means **given that**. Read the information after it first: “restrict attention to low-engagement customers.” Then ask for the event before it within that restricted group.

## A conditional distribution lists every remaining possibility

Let $X=0$ mean retained and $X=1$ mean churned. Given low engagement, its complete distribution is

| Status | Conditional probability |
|---|---|
| Retained | $5/20=0.25$ |
| Churned | $15/20=0.75$ |

The entries add to one because they account for all 20 customers remaining after the restriction. Churn is still uncertain; knowing engagement does not tell us the status with certainty.

## Relate this to the original population

In the original population, the relevant joint probabilities are $0.05$ and $0.15$, whose sum is $0.20$. To turn them into probabilities within the selected group, divide both by the same group total:

$$
\frac{0.05}{0.20}=0.25,
\qquad
\frac{0.15}{0.20}=0.75.
$$

This common rescaling is called **normalization**: the selected group becomes the whole population for the new question, so its probabilities must add to one.

Conditioning describes what our information does to the probability calculation. It does not claim that observing engagement caused anyone to churn, or that a customer's past outcome changed when we learned the engagement tier.
