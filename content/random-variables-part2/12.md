# Read the audit's joint probability table without losing precision

In a uniformly chosen sample of 7 images from 25, $X$ counts minor errors and $Y$ counts completely mislabeled images. There are 8, 5, and 12 available images in the minor-error, completely mislabeled, and perfect categories. The source table evaluates $p(x,y)=\binom8x\binom5y\binom{12}{7-x-y}/480700$.

## Locate a cell using both labels

In the source table, **columns give $X$ and rows give $Y$**. The entry in column $X=2$, row $Y=1$, is $p(2,1)\approx0.1442$.

That is the probability of exactly 2 minor-error images **and** exactly 1 completely mislabeled image. It does not include samples with two minor errors and a different value of $Y$.

## An exact version of the table

Every cell has the same denominator, $\binom{25}7=480700$. The following entries are **counts of favorable samples**; divide any entry by 480700 to obtain its probability.

| $Y\backslash X$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ | $4$ | $5$ | $6$ | $7$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| $0$ | 792 | 7392 | 22176 | 27720 | 15400 | 3696 | 336 | 8 |
| $1$ | 4620 | 31680 | 69300 | 61600 | 23100 | 3360 | 140 | 0 |
| $2$ | 7920 | 39600 | 61600 | 36960 | 8400 | 560 | 0 | 0 |
| $3$ | 4950 | 17600 | 18480 | 6720 | 700 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| $4$ | 1100 | 2640 | 1680 | 280 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| $5$ | 66 | 96 | 28 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The positive entries form a truncated triangle: $y$ can run only through 5, and within row $y$ we need $x\le7-y$. There are $8+7+6+5+4+3=33$ possible pairs, with different probabilities.

The source's four-decimal values are consistent with these counts. In particular,

$$p(7,0)=\frac8{480700}\approx0.00001664<0.0001.$$

It is positive even though rounding it to four decimal places would display $0.0000$. Use the support or the exact formula to decide whether an event is impossible.

## Sum a row: no completely mislabeled images

The event $Y=0$ allows every possible minor-error count in its row. Hence

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(Y=0)
&=\frac{792+7392+22176+27720+15400+3696+336+8}{480700}\\
&=\frac{77520}{480700}\\
&\approx\boxed{0.16126482.}
\end{aligned}
$$

We can check this directly. There are $12+8=20$ images that are not completely mislabeled, so every allowed sample chooses all seven from those 20:

$$P(Y=0)=\frac{\binom{20}7}{\binom{25}7}=\frac{77520}{480700}.$$

The row sum and the direct count describe the same event at different levels of detail.

## Sum a diagonal: exactly three imperfect images

Both error categories count as imperfect. The event $X+Y=3$ consists of four pairs:

$$ (3,0),\ (2,1),\ (1,2),\ (0,3). $$

Thus

$$
\begin{aligned}
P(X+Y=3)
&=\frac{27720+69300+39600+4950}{480700}\\
&=\frac{141570}{480700}\approx\boxed{0.29450801.}
\end{aligned}
$$

There are 13 imperfect images altogether. Choosing 3 of those and 4 of the 12 perfect images gives the same count:

$$\binom{13}3\binom{12}4=286\cdot495=141570.$$
