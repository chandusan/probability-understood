# Solve the rectangular integration exercise

Evaluate $\int_0^2\int_0^1xy^2\,dy\,dx$. The problem specifies both the surface $xy^2$ and the rectangle $0\le x\le2$, $0\le y\le1$.

## Read the order before calculating

The inner differential is $dy$, so integrate $y$ first. Hold $x$ fixed and use the power rule:

$$
\int_0^1xy^2\,dy
=x\left[\frac{y^3}{3}\right]_0^1
=\frac x3.
$$

Now integrate over the outer range:

$$
\int_0^2\frac x3\,dx
=\left[\frac{x^2}{6}\right]_0^2
=\boxed{\frac23}.
$$

The full calculation is therefore

$$
\boxed{\int_0^2\int_0^1xy^2\,dy\,dx=\frac23.}
$$

## Check by reversing the order

Describe the same rectangle with $x$ as the inner variable:

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1\int_0^2xy^2\,dx\,dy
&=\int_0^1\left[\frac{x^2y^2}{2}\right]_0^2dy\\
&=\int_0^1 2y^2\,dy
=\frac23.
\end{aligned}
$$

The two orders agree.

## Check by separating the factors

Because the region is a rectangle and the integrand factors,

$$
\left(\int_0^2x\,dx\right)
\left(\int_0^1y^2\,dy\right)
=2\cdot\frac13=\frac23.
$$

This is the same repeated integration written compactly.

<details markdown="1"><summary>What changes if the inner upper limit is x instead of 1?</summary>

The region becomes $0\le x\le2$, $0\le y\le x$, a triangle. Then the inner integral is $x[x^3/3]=x^4/3$, and the total is $\int_0^2x^4/3\,dx=32/15$. Changing a limit changes the region and therefore the answer.

</details>
