CDFs, endpoints & jumps · Slide 28

Solve a discrete table by selecting the values in each event

A probability table gives masses at specific values, not a continuous spread across the gaps between them. Completing the table and reading its inequalities carefully solves all four parts of this example.

~2 min reading/Source + intuition + practice
Let’s understand it

State the distribution and the questions

The random variable $X$ has exactly four possible values:

$x$ 3 5 8 10
$p(x)=P(X=x)$ $0.3$ $0.2$ $0.3$ unknown

We want (a) $P(X=10)$, (b) $P(X\ge5)$, (c) $P(X>5)$, and (d) $P(4<X\le8)$. Values not listed in the table have probability zero. In particular, $P(X=4)=P(X=6)=P(X=7)=0$.

Part (a): account for all probability

The four singleton events are disjoint and exhaustive. Their probabilities must therefore sum to one:

$$ p(3)+p(5)+p(8)+p(10)=1. $$

The three known masses add to $0.3+0.2+0.3=0.8$, leaving

$$ \boxed{P(X=10)=1-0.8=0.2.} $$

This is valid because the problem says the table describes the whole distribution. If additional unlisted outcomes were possible with positive probability, the missing entry could not be determined from this information alone.

Part (b): “at least five” includes five

The event $X\ge5$ includes $5$, $8$, and $10$. Its probability is

$$ \boxed{P(X\ge5)=p(5)+p(8)+p(10) =0.2+0.3+0.2=0.7.} $$

The complement contains only three, giving the independent check $1-p(3)=1-0.3=0.7$. Notice that three qualifying values out of four does not give a probability of $3/4$: the four values are not equally likely.

Part (c): “greater than five” excludes five

Now the qualifying values are only $8$ and $10$:

$$ \boxed{P(X>5)=p(8)+p(10)=0.3+0.2=0.5.} $$

Equivalently, remove the mass at five from the answer to part (b):

$$ P(X>5)=P(X\ge5)-P(X=5)=0.7-0.2=0.5. $$

The change from $\ge$ to $>$ changes the answer by exactly $0.2$, the probability of the endpoint that was removed.

Part (d): check both boundaries separately

For $4<X\le8$, the value must be strictly above four and at most eight. Five and eight satisfy both conditions; three fails the lower condition, and ten fails the upper one. Thus

$$ \boxed{P(4<X\le8)=p(5)+p(8)=0.2+0.3=0.5.} $$

The interval contains many real numbers, but only two belong to the support. Its length, $8-4=4$, has no direct role in the answer.

Compare the selected masses

Question Qualifying values Probability
$X=10$ $10$ $0.2$
$X\ge5$ $5,8,10$ $0.7$
$X>5$ $8,10$ $0.5$
$4<X\le8$ $5,8$ $0.5$

The final two events have equal probabilities but contain different outcomes. Equal probability does not mean equal events.

Check your understanding

Would replacing $4<X\le8$ with $4\le X<8$ change the answer?

Identify what enters and what leaves

Including four adds nothing because $p(4)=0$. Excluding eight removes $p(8)=0.3$. The new event contains only five, so its probability is $0.5-0.3=0.2$. An endpoint change matters precisely when it adds or removes positive mass.
