How to read this audit

Each row links to the relevant lesson section. Coverage means an explanation of the mathematical content and a worked treatment where appropriate; it does not mean a word-for-word slide copy. Decorative images are not reproduced. Where a plot lacks underlying data, its reasoning is explained without inventing precise observations. Source errors and ambiguous conventions are discussed in the lessons.

Open the worked-example panels as you study: the main narrative alone is intentionally shorter than the full slide companion.
