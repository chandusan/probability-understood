STAT 5701 midterm exam, Fall 2025. Six questions covering joint distributions, continuous and discrete random variables, event probabilities, and conditional probability.

Work through the questions at your own pace. Open “Questions with solutions” when you are ready to check your reasoning.

01

A joint continuous density

Let $X$ and $Y$ be two continuous random variables with a joint pdf given by

$$ f(x,y)=cx^2y(1+y),\qquad 0\le x\le3,\quad 0\le y\le3. $$

The density is zero outside this region.

(a) · 6 points

Find the value of $c$ that makes this a valid density function.

(b) · 6 points

Find the marginal pdf $f_X(x)$.

02

A continuous density on an unbounded interval

Let the probability density function of a continuous random variable $X$ be given by

$$ f(x)=\frac{c}{x^3},\qquad x\ge1. $$

The density is zero for $x<1$.

(a) · 6 points

Find $c$. (If you can't find $c$, assume $c=1$ and move on.)

(b) · 6 points

Find the cumulative distribution function $F(x)$.

(c) · 5 points

Find $P(2<X<3)$.

(d) · Bonus, +5 points

Does the moment generating function of $X$ exist? Explain why or why not.

03

Events in four coin tosses

A fair coin is tossed four times, and the resulting sequence of heads and tails is recorded. Define the following events:

  • $A=\{\text{exactly two heads appear}\}$;
  • $B=\{\text{heads and tails alternate}\}$;
  • $C=\{\text{the last toss is tails}\}$.

Assume tosses are independent and each toss is equally likely to be tails or heads. Find the following probabilities:

(a) · 5 points

$P(B\cap\overline C)$.

(b) · 5 points

$P(A\mid C)$.

(c) · 5 points

$P(\overline B\cup\overline C)$.

04

Complete and use a joint probability table

Let $X$ and $Y$ be two discrete random variables whose joint and marginal distributions are partially given in the following table:

$X\backslash Y$ $1$ $2$ $3$ $p_X(x)$
$1$ $1/6$ $0$ — $1/3$
$2$ — $1/4$ — $1/3$
$3$ — — $1/4$ —
$p_Y(y)$ $1/6$ $1/3$ — —

(a) · 6 points

Complete the table.

(b) · 5 points

Find $P(X=Y)$.

(c) · 5 points

Find $E(Y)$.

(d) · 5 points

Determine the conditional pmf $p(x\mid y=2)$.

05

A discrete distribution and its summaries

A discrete random variable $X$ has the following probability distribution:

$x$ $1$ $2$ $3$ $4$
$p(x)$ $0.5$ $0.2$ $0.1$ $0.2$

(a) · 6 points

Find the cumulative distribution function $F(x)$.

(b) · 6 points

Find $\operatorname{Var}(X)$.

(c) · 5 points

Find the mgf $m_X(t)$.

06

A noisy binary communication channel

A message is transmitted as a binary code of $0$s and $1$s. Experience with the equipment being used suggests that if a $0$ is sent, it will be received as $0$ $94\%$ of the time. If a $1$ is sent, it will be received as a $1$ $95\%$ of the time (and as a $0$ $5\%$ of the time). The text being sent is known to be $7\%$ $1$s and $93\%$ $0$s.

(a) · 6 points

What is the probability that the received message is $1$?

(b) · 6 points

Suppose the next signal sent is received as a $1$. What is the probability it was sent as a $0$?

(c) · 6 points

If the same message is sent twice, and assuming the received messages are conditionally independent, what is the probability that it is received as two $1$s?
