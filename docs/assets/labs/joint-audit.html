Count image samples, then turn the counts into probabilities

Draw 7 distinct images uniformly without replacement from 25: 12 perfectly labeled, 8 with minor errors, and 5 garbage images. X counts minor errors and Y counts garbage images. Each unordered sample of 7 images is equally likely.

Click a table cell to inspect that exact pair. Every positive cell shows an exact sample count, even when its probability is tiny. Stripes and “impossible” mean an exact zero; darker shading means greater probability. An outlined cell or region marks the selected event.
