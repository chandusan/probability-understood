Some users spend nothing; others have continuously varying spending

X is spending in hundreds of dollars. A fraction m spends exactly zero. The remaining fraction 1 − m has conditional density x/2 on 0 < x < 2. Its contribution to the whole population is (1 − m)x/2, equal to 0.3x when m = 40%.

The jump at zero has height m. The curve then accumulates continuous probability.

The area under this density is 1 − m. The separate point mass m completes the total probability.
