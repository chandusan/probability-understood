One triangle, three different probability questions

This separate, simple model has constant joint density f(x,y) = 2 inside 0 < y < x < 1 and zero elsewhere. The triangle has area 1/2, so its total probability is 2 × 1/2 = 1.

The highlighted triangle is the event X ≤ x. The vertical line shows the possible Y values when X is fixed at x. A line has zero area; conditional density is obtained by normalizing a slice, rather than dividing by the probability of that exact line.
