Build events, then negate their union or intersection

The sample space is {1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}. Tick A, B, or C to put an outcome in that event. Every card stays in the sample space, even if no boxes are ticked.

A thick border and “included” label mark the outcomes in both resulting sets. The two sides always agree; they are different descriptions of the same membership rule.
